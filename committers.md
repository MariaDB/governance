# Committers

## **Committers and Reviewers**

This document describes rules and guidelines for committers and reviewers. The Committer and Reviewer roles are defined in [Governance](governance.md); this text describes how they work.

### **What commit rights mean**

Commit rights mean a technical ability to push to MariaDB repositories. They do not give a permission to bypass a review requirement — a committer has no approval right of their own. A committer is required to keep the Jira tickets for their work up to date.

### **Becoming a committer or a reviewer**

Committer or reviewer status is sponsored by one or more maintainers.

### **Ceasing to be a committer or a reviewer**

A committer ceases to hold commit rights by stepping down, by sustained inactivity, when the sponsorship is revoked, or for cause, on the same basis as maintainers ([Ceasing to be a maintainer](governance.md#22-ceasing-to-be-a-maintainer)).

### **Updating this file**

Grants, removals, and moves between this file are recorded once confirmed under the sponsorship rule above.
